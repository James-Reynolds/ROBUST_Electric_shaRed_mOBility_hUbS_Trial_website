The ROBUST app is available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/robust-mobility-hubs/id6755043620). 


![An image showing various menu and other screens that are part of the ROBUST app ](assets\img\ROBUST_app.png)



Vehicle bookings can be made (e-bikes) through the ESB eHub [iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/esb-ehub/id1612495746) and [Andriod app]( https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sharebike.android.esb&hl=e), and (electric cars) via
the [Enterprise Car Club](https://www.enterprisecarclub.ie/ie/en/programs/promotion/esb-hub.html).
