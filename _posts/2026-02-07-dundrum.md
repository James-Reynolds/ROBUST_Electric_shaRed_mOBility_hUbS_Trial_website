# Dundrum hub

The Dundrum hub is currently under construction. It [is located within the Sandyford Road Car Park](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=17/53.288007/-6.241211) , a short distance from the Dundrum Town Centre shopping centre, and from the Balally and Dundrum stations on the Luas Green Line

![A map showing the location of the Dundrum ROBUST hub site at 53.29 North, 6.24 West and an indicative walking catchment area indicating that the hub is an approximately 800 metres south east of the Dundrum Luas station and 600 metres north west of Balally station.](assets\img\DLR_2_cropped.png "Dundrum hub location and catchment area")
