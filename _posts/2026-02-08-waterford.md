# Waterford hub

The Waterford hub was [launched on October 31st, 2025,](https://waterfordcouncil.ie/new-emobility-ehub-launches-in-ballybricken-waterford/) launched by Mayor of Waterford City and County, Cllr. Seamus Ryan. It [centrally located in Ballybricken,](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=19/52.259938/-7.117167) within 15 minutes walking distance of the main railway station.
