# Galway hub


The Galway hub, [located in Westside,](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=19/53.277346/-9.073489)  was [launched on March 21, 2025](https://www.atu.ie/news/one-of-irelands-first-emobility-ehub-established-in-westside-galway-serving-west-serving-west-and-northwest).  The hub is located immediately beside the Westside Library, the Westside Resource Centre and the Westside Community Centre.  It is on the opposite side of Seamus Quirke Road from Aldi, and within 10 to 15 minutes walk of the University Hospital Galway and the University of Galway.
