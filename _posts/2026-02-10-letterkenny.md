# Letterkenny hub

The Letterkenny hub was [launched on September 5th, 2025](https://www.atu.ie/news/new-sustainable-transport-ehub-opens-in-letterkenny). 


## Sign up 
![An image of the Letterkenny signup information](assets/img/Letterkenny-poster-1_260218.png)


## Location
The hub is located centrally on Justice Walsh Road, close to the intersection with Kinnear Lane.  The hub is immediately outside the [Letterkenny Primary Care Centre](https://www2.hse.ie/services/primary-care-centres/letterkenny-primary-care-centre/), an 11 minute walk from the Letterkenny Shopping Centre on Port Road, and around a 15 minute walk from the Letterkenny University Hospital or the ATU campus.
